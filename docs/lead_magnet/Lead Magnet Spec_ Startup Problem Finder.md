# **Lead Magnet Landing Page Specification: Startup Problem Finder**

**Context for Coding Agent:** This document outlines the structure, interactive elements, and copywriting for a lead magnet landing page. The audience is coming directly from an Instagram Reel (Episode 1 of "Build a Startup in 30 Days") where the core message was debunking the "I need a 100% unique idea" myth and shifting the focus to finding a profitable problem. Please apply the existing design system guidelines and practical content layout conventions to this structure.

## **1\. Technical & UI Guidelines**

> * **Design System:** Apply established brand colors, typography, and component libraries.  
> * **Language:** The UI text should primarily be in Arabic (RTL layout) to match the audience from the IG Reel, with English terms where appropriate for tech jargon.  
> * **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first design is critical (95%+ of traffic will be from the Instagram mobile app).

## **2\. Page Architecture & Copywriting**

### **Section 1: Hero Banner**

**Goal:** Validate the user's click from IG and restate the core premise immediately.

> * **Headline (H1):** سر الـ 90% من الـ Startups الناجحة: المشكلة قبل الفكرة (The secret of 90% of successful startups: The problem before the idea).  
> * **Subheadline:** متضيعش وقتك في تدوير على فكرة عبقرية. اتعلم إزاي تلاقي "وجع" حقيقي تبني عليه البيزنس بتاعك. (Don't waste time looking for a genius idea. Learn how to find a real "pain" to build your business on.)  
> * **Primary CTA:** Anchor link to the MCQ / Tool section. Button Text: ابدأ الاختبار دلوقتي (Start the Quiz Now).

### **Section 3: Interactive Component (MCQ)**

**Goal:** Engage the user and test their understanding of the "Mindset" before giving them the tool.  
**Component Type:** Interactive Quiz Card. When the user clicks an option, show immediate visual feedback (Green for correct, Red for incorrect) and a tooltip explanation.

> * **Question:** إيه أحسن طريقة تبدأ بيها الـ Startup بتاعتك؟ (What is the best way to start your startup?)  
> * **Option A (Incorrect):** أقعد أعصف ذهني لحد ما أجيب فكرة متعملتش في الكوكب قبل كده. (Brainstorm until I find an idea never done on the planet.)  
  * *Feedback state:* ❌ غلط\! ده الفخ اللي بيقع فيه 90% من الناس. (Wrong\! This is the trap 90% of people fall into.)  
> * **Option B (Correct):** أدور على مشكلة بتعاني منها شريحة معينة ومفيش حل مريح ليهم. (Look for a problem a specific segment suffers from with no convenient solution.)  
  * *Feedback state:* ✅ صح جداً\! البيزنس بيتبني على حل المشاكل. (Exactly\! Businesses are built on solving problems.)  
> * **Option C (Incorrect):** أخد فكرة ناجحة في أمريكا وأعملها كوبي بيست في مصر. (Copy-paste a successful American idea in Egypt.)  
  * *Feedback state:* ❌ مش دايماً بتنجح\! لأن المشاكل وثقافة الناس بتختلف من مكان للتاني. (Doesn't always work\! Problems and culture differ from place to place.)

### **Section 4: Actionable Steps using Startup Blueprint**

**Goal:** Step-by-step guide to using the requested tool to validate their problem.  
*(Note to Dev: Create this as a vertical timeline UI or stepped cards)*

> 1. **Step 1: حدد الشريحة بتاعتك (Identify your segment)**  
>    Don't target "everyone." Pick a group you understand (e.g., freelancers, college students, working moms). Identify what keeps them awake at night.  
> 2. **Step 2: افتح أداة التخطيط (Open the planning tool)**  
>    Go to [StartupBlueprint.dev](https://www.startupblueprint.dev/). This tool will help you map out the problem systematically instead of just guessing.  
> 3. **Step 3: املأ الفجوة (Fill the Gap)**  
>    Use the tool to map out your competitors. Find the "Gap" (الفجوة) — the specific pain point that big companies are too slow or too big to care about.  
> 4. **Step 4: اختبر "وجع" المشكلة (Validate the "Pain")**  
>    Use the validation frameworks on Startup Blueprint to see if people are actually willing to pay to have this problem solved, or if it's just a minor inconvenience.

### **Section 5: Final Call to Action (Lead Capture)**

**Goal:** Capture the email in exchange for a PDF cheat sheet or template.

> * **Heading:** جاهز تطبق عملي؟ (Ready to apply this practically?)  
> * **Body text:** نزل الـ Problem-Finder Canvas (مجاناً) وامشي على الخطوات عشان تطلع بأول 3 مشاكل تقدر تبني عليهم فكرتك. (Download the Problem-Finder Canvas for free and follow the steps to find the first 3 problems you can build your idea on.)  
> * **Form Fields:** Name, Email Address.  
> * **Submit Button:** ابعتلي الـ Canvas (Send me the Canvas).
